Welcome to Satya Sai Tutorial Digital Portal

Satya Sai Tutorial sirph ek coaching institute nahi hai, balki Ganjam ke hazaron bacchon ke sapnon, umeedon aur unke maa-baap ke vishwas ka ek pavitra mandir hai. Saal 2014 mein apni sthapna (created) se lekar ab tak, humne shiksha ke kshetra mein ek behad bhavuk (emotional) aur gauravshali safar taye kiya hai. Khallikote aur Beguniapada ke chhote-chhote kasbon ke un mehnati students ko sahara dena hamara kartavya raha hai, jo aage badhne ka jazba to rakhte hain par sahi raste se anjaan hote hain. Humne pichle saalon mein sirph kitabi gyaan nahi banta, balki bacchon ke andar anushasan, sanskar aur 'Human Excellence' ki buniyaad ko majboot kiya hai taaki wo har pariksha mein safal ho sakein.

Aaj hamara ye digital platform usi purane vishwas aur naye daur ki shuruat hai, jahan bache bina kisi pareshani ke ghar baithe behtareen notes aur library ka labh utha sakte hain. Hum har bache ki aankhon mein chupe sapnon ko samajhte hain aur unhe sach karne ke liye hamesha tatpar hain.

🏆 OUR PROUD HISTORY (GAURAVSHALI ITIHAS)

Hamein ye batate hue behad gaurav aur khushi hoti hai ki Saal 2016 mein hamare coaching se hi District Topper student nikla tha! Ye aitihasik parinaam hamari hard-work, sahi rasta dikhane ki kshamta aur bacchon ki kadi mehnat ka saboot hai jo ab tak lagatar jaari hai.

Rules & Regulations - Session 2025-26

1. General Rules

  • A neat and clean and Formal dress must be worn at all time.
  • Students should be Polite, Punctual and Regular.
  • Students should complete all "Home works" in given time to them and attend all Tests/Exams.
  • Students should reach the centre at least 5 minutes before the Class Time starts.
  • Students should bring all "Necessary Materials" as for their study use (text/workbooks etc.)
  • Cell Phones/Mobile should be "Switched Off or Silent Mode" inside the classroom.
  • Parents can discuss the progress of their words every month by "Prior Appointment."
  • Any damages made by the students be chargeable as Compensation.

2. Parents Teacher Meeting

Must Be held On Last Sunday of Every Month At-11 am.

3. Discipline

The Observance of Rules of Discipline and Good Behaviour Shall Be a precondition to a student's continuance in the institute.

4. Absence

Students must not remain absent without any satisfactory Reasons; in every case a letter from the parents/guardian must be submitted on the day pupil rejoins.

5. Special classes/Extra classes

  • Special Classes for Pupils, considered to be in need of Extra Classes are held in the Institute Premises.
  • Pupils must be informed about the special classes by the "Notice".
  • No any extra fees will be charged for the "Special or Extra Classes."

6. Weekly seminar classes

Student bound to attend.

7. Responsibility

"Your Tutorial, Your Responsibility."

8. Celebrations and Ceremonies

Ganesh Puja, Saraswati Puja, Welcome Ceremony for the juniors and Farewell ceremony for the seniors, Independence Day, Republic Day, Teachers Day and Children's Day will be observed.

N.B. All students must have to attend in these Celebrations and Ceremonies as well as co-operate us after giving their 100% efforts.

9. Different Competitions on Memorial Days/Special Occasions

  • Essay Competitions & Debate Competitions
  • Song Competitions & One-act Play Competitions
  • Jhoti Competitions, Mimicry, Drawing Competitions, Dance Competitions etc.

10. Different Sports and Events (On the Eve of Children's Day)

Events include: Cricket (Men's), Kabaddi, Khokho, Badminton, Running, Long Jump, High Jump.

11. I.Q. Test

An I.Q test in written will be taken on 2nd Saturday of every month.

12. Exam Schedule

  • Weekly Exam will be conducting every month.
  • Objective Type Exams (Will be conduct every week)
  • Semi V.S.T Exams (After complete 30% Course)
  • 7 Crash Course Exams (After complete 70% course)
  • Two V.S.T Exams (After complete 100% course)
